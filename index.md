## Quarter 2 Project Proposal Brainstorming

**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic this quarter has been learning HVAC systems themselves: getting familiar with how the equipment works, the terminology technicians use on the job, and how that specialized vocabulary maps onto the parts and troubleshooting information a conversational assistant would need to retrieve.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I'd like to investigate how wearables and voice interfaces could be incorporated into the support tool, so that technicians could interact with the system hands-free while working on equipment.

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

I would want more frequent meetings with mentors, smaller group sizes, and more time actually working on the product itself rather than on planning and process. The bulk of this quarter has been planning and process, so I would like to focus on building next quarter

**What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in exploring user-centered design techniques, particularly usability testing and iterative prototyping. Since our project focuses on improving the experience of technicians using Bosch's support chatbot, I think it would be valuable to test different interface designs and observe how users interact with them.
